CiteSpect

Research Paper Citation Analysis System

Enter a paper's DOI or OpenAlex Work ID to inspect the papers it cites. CiteSpect detects Self-Citations (shared OpenAlex author IDs via a C99 Hash Table with Separate Chaining) and Reciprocal Cross-Citations (mutual citations A → B and B → A via Adjacency-List Graph traversal).

    What the categories mean

    Self-Citation (SELF / NOT IDENTIFIED AS SELF)
    The citing and cited papers share at least one exact OpenAlex author ID in the C99 Separate-Chaining Hash Table.
    Confirmed Reciprocal Citation (CONFIRMED RECIPROCAL)
    Paper A cites Paper B (A → B) and Paper B's outgoing reference graph also cites Paper A (B → A).
    Not Reciprocal (NOT RECIPROCAL)
    Paper B's outgoing reference list was retrieved and verified, and no reverse citation edge (B → A) exists.
    Reciprocal Unknown (UNKNOWN)
    Paper B's reference metadata is unavailable or could not be fetched from OpenAlex, so reciprocity cannot be verified.

    Self-citation is common when authors build on their own earlier work. Unmatched author lists are labeled Not Identified as Self because missing author IDs cannot prove cross-citation.
